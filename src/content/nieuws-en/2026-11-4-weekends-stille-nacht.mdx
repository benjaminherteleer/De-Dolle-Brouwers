<Artikel label="Stille Nacht 2026" titel="The mad Christmas beer is here." sub="For sale at the brewery, for four weekends.">

<Cijfers items={[[11, 'brews this year'], ['8 May', 'first brew'], ['12%', 'alcohol']]} />

After months of preparation it is finally here. We made 11 brews this year, and the first one dates back to 8 May.

These brews don't go into the bottle one by one. We always blend two consecutive brews and bottle them together. That way you can tell which bottling you are holding: the cap has one star for the first bottling, two stars for the second, and so on.

Stille Nacht is 12% and evolves over time. <Markeer>The bitterness decreases</Markeer>, so it can be worth keeping a bottle.

</Artikel>
