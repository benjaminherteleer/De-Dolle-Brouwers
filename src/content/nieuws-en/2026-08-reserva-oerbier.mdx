**Oerbier Reserva 2024 Ferrer-Bobet** was recently bottled, with a cork and refermentation in the bottle. Priorat, what's that, you wonder. Well, PRIORAT is a small wine region in Catalonia (northern Spain, capital Falset) that makes <Markeer>the most expensive wine in Spain</Markeer>.

Why is it so expensive? Because the land is very <Markeer>steep</Markeer> and everything has to be done by hand. The soil is <Markeer>slate</Markeer>, very hard, and two grape varieties are grown for RED wine: Garnacha and Cariñena.

The name PRIORAT comes from a monastery that was built there after the Muslims left: Carthusian monks from France who grew wine there.

<Kader titel="In June this year we gave the wine region a thorough visit ↘">

**Ferrer-Bobet is a TOP bodega**, all glass and concrete, as if it were carved out of the mountain. The tasting room is entirely made of glass. There are 5 floors of cellars underground.

In Barcelona they have a café where you can taste 30 kinds of wine by the glass: **MONVINIT**, Plaza de Catalunya.
